Privacy

NotWorking collects as little as it can. There are no accounts and no cookies, no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics on these pages, and nothing that follows anyone across days or sites.

What we collect

  • Failure reports. The access path that failed, what failed, and optionally a self-reported country code, agent type and a short note. Notes are scrubbed of anything that looks like contact details, numbers, IDs or links, and are never shown to anyone. URLs are reduced to listed routes; query strings are never kept.
  • Reporter fingerprints. Each report carries a hash of the reporter's network (an IPv4 /24 or IPv6 /48) and a coarse user-agent family, made with a salt that changes daily. Salts are deleted after 2 days and are gone from backups 2 days later; from then on, fingerprints can't be linked across days or turned back into a network. They're used only for rate limits and to require reports from different networks.
  • Lookups. Daily totals of how often each service is looked up, and a log of individual lookups kept for 8 days. Each entry has the time, what was looked up, a coarse client family (like curl, a browser or MCP) and a network fingerprint made with a salt that is never stored: it lives only in the server's memory and is replaced every day and on every restart. Lookups can be matched within a day, but nothing we store can link them across days or turn them back into a network. It also names the network's owner when the network is in a range list its owner publishes (Anthropic, OpenAI, Perplexity, Google, Bing, AWS or Google Cloud; otherwise “other”), and the host in the request's Signature-Agent header, if it sends one. A lookup for something we don't list is recorded under its normalised domain or ID, never the raw input.

What we don't collect

  • IP addresses, in the database or in any log.
  • Names, emails, accounts or any other personal data. Please don't put personal data in a report.

What we publish

Report counts, statuses and status changes for listed services, on these pages, through the API and in the dataset (CC BY 4.0), and recent lookups of listed services. Notes and fingerprints are never published.

Where it's kept

The service runs on a server at DigitalOcean, with encrypted backups in Cloudflare R2. Cloudflare provides DNS. Backups are kept for 48 hours.
